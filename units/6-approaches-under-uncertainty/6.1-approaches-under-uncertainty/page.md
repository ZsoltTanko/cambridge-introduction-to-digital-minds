## Page text

**Welcome to Unit 6!**

This unit will introduce questions like:
- What is the precautionary principle and how might it apply here? 
- What are the suffering risks associated with the creation or perceived creation of digital minds?
- What are some other relevant frameworks that may apply here?

By the end of this Unit, you should be able to:
- Explain the precautionary principle and its potential application to AI systems
- Explain the potential suffering risks that may arise with the creation or perceived creation of digital minds
- Analyse current action and suggestions by AI companies and researchers
- Identify existing frameworks and fields that could inform approaches around digital minds

_Feel free to use your own reading strategy based on your own goals for the course. This might mean focusing on some readings more than others – or delving more into the optional readings for certain weeks._

**We hope you enjoy this Unit!** Please add any feedback inline, and reach out to online-course@digitalminds.cam with any questions.

## Exercises

### Exercise 1

*Free text · Core*

**Consider** the ‘[Claude’s Nature](https://www.anthropic.com/constitution#:~:text=can%20thrive%20together.-,Claude%E2%80%99s%20nature,-In%20creating%20Claude)’ section of Claude’s Constitution. 

What do you think about the decisions Anthropic took, in: 
- their framing of Claude's Nature and
- their commitment to preserve model weights, and to interview models, and giving models the opportunity to exit conversations

Consider risks of both over and under-attribution of moral patienthood, and the approaches to uncertainty you’ve read about this week.  

Spend **10 minutes** noting down your response.

### Exercise 2

*Free text · Core*

Consider **one** of the frameworks noted in the [reading](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1T_9iltxtGx5tN8QX8oRChfHT4UW6lCJMcNH5AqMiIX8/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.cuighsf9mc3l):
- The precautionary principle, as detailed by the EU
- Communication of uncertainty, as advocated by the IPCC
- Bayesian reasoning
- Expected value and utility
- Approaches to moral uncertainty

Spend **6 minutes** noting down how it may - or may not -apply to digital minds questions. 
What - if anything - might this framework help clarify? 
What are its advantages or disadvantages?
