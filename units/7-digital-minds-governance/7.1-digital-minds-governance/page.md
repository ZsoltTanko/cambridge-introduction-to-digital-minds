## Page text

**Welcome to Unit 7!**

This unit will introduce questions like:
- What are different approaches to digital minds governance? 
- What are current legislative approaches, and what policy frameworks and fields might be relevant?
- Which rights and duties may apply to digital systems, and what are arguments for and against granting them?

By the end of this Unit, you should be able to:
- Analyse current regulatory action around digital minds concerns
- List potential rights (positive/negative, private/public) and liabilities that could apply to digital minds
- Discuss the potential application of these rights and liabilities to digital minds
- Identify existing frameworks and fields that could inform the regulation of digital minds
- Discuss potential priorities for digital minds governance
- Appraise the arguments for and against a moratorium or ban on creating sentient (or seemingly sentient) AI systems

**We hope you enjoy this Unit!** Please add any feedback inline, and reach out to online-course@digitalminds.cam with any questions.
