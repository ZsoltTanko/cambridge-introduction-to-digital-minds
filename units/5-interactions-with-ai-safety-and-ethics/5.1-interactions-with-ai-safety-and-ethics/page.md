## Page text

**Welcome to Unit 5!**

This unit will introduce questions like:
- What are the AI safety, AI ethics and AI welfare fields, and how may they align or conflict? 
- What are potential safety risks associated with the creation (or perceived creation) of digital minds? 

Learning outcomes:
- Describe the fields of AI safety, AI ethics and AI welfare
- Understand the relationship between AI safety and AI welfare, including areas of alignment and potential conflict
- Identify interventions that could potentially influence AI welfare
- Recognise the interplay between AI safety and perceptions of digital minds

## Exercises

### Exercise 1

*Free text · Core*

Note down **three AI Safety or Ethics risks** that concern you. If you’re looking for inspiration, take a look at this [summary table](https://airisk.mit.edu/#:~:text=full%20database.-,Domain%20Taxonomy%20of%20AI%20Risks,-The%20Domain%20Taxonomy) from MIT’s AI Risk Repository. **Spend 10 minutes considering**:
- How do you rank AI welfare against these risks?
- To what extent may AI welfare exacerbate or alleviate these risks?
- How may perceptions of AI consciousness interact with these risks? _It might be useful to consider the factors that Register et al highlight as relevant to privacy concerns._

### Exercise 2

*Free text · Core*

Consider **potential AI Welfare interventions**, such as:

- a commitment not to delete model weights
- a commitment to protect an AI model from harm (e.g. prevent actions that cause distress representations to activate beyond a certain level)

**Brainstorm** ways in which a superintelligent AI system's **motivations or implicit goals** might - or might not - shift with these measures.
