## Key concepts & terminology

- **Moral standing / moral status** - whether, and how much, a being's interests or claims count morally. The page's unit questions and the learning objective "define key terms… relating to consciousness, sentience and moral standing" point to this usage.
- **Moral patienthood** - being an entity toward which others can have moral duties. Used in exercise 1 on the page.
- **Routes to moral standing** - the grounds on which a being might have moral standing (for example consciousness, sentience, agency). The guide names this as a main topic.
- **Super-human moral status** - the possibility that an AI system's moral status or claims could exceed a typical human's. Named in the guide.
- **Individual and collective significance** - the section title the guide mentions. Read as: the moral importance of one system versus many systems or society as a whole.
- **Over- and under-attribution** - the page's framing of two errors: crediting moral standing to systems that lack it, and denying it to systems that have it.
