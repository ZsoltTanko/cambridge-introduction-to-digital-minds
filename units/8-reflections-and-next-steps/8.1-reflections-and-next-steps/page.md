## Page text

**Welcome to Unit 8!**

This unit is your opportunity to consolidate what you’ve learnt on this course, and make a contribution to your group. Much of the material for this week is focussed in the **brainstorming doc in the written prompt**. 

We will cover:
- What are the main organisations involved in digital minds work, and how might you get involved?
- What are you taking away from this course? 
- What 4 minute contribution would you like to share with your group?

By the end of this Unit, you should be able to:
- Identify relevant organisations and institutions working in and around digital minds
- Examine current work and research on digital minds and identify gaps
- Analyse principles for research on digital minds
- Articulate your thinking around digital minds questions

**We hope you enjoy this Unit!** Please add any feedback inline, and reach out to online-course@digitalminds.cam with any questions.
