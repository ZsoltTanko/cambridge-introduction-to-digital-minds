## Page text

**Welcome to Unit 3!**

This unit will introduce questions like:
- How might we test for AI consciousness and sentience? How do different methods compare?
- Why and how might we test for agency and other mental phenomena, such as belief and desires?

By the end of this unit, you should be able to:
- Appraise methods for evaluating consciousness in AI systems
- Describe other mental states we might test for, and their relevance
- Analyse current empirical research and its relevance to consciousness and other mental states
- Describe challenges with individuating or identifying digital minds

**We hope you enjoy this Unit!** Please add any feedback inline, and reach out to online-course@digitalminds.cam with any questions.

### Exercise 1

*Free text · Core*

Choose **one** of the categories of approaches to evaluating consciousness and other mental states detailed **in this [doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ijCseQ79SlLOi_7i38WbyzUYNfy_l-jErmRncLuKxqk/edit?tab=t.0).**

You might choose **one** of:

- **[Theory-based approaches to evaluating consciousness](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ijCseQ79SlLOi_7i38WbyzUYNfy_l-jErmRncLuKxqk/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.mnw6gfj02xmn)**: _Use theories of consciousness to inform consciousness evaluations and identify indicators_
- **[Approaches based on self-report](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ijCseQ79SlLOi_7i38WbyzUYNfy_l-jErmRncLuKxqk/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.2hdblwz619ez):** _Use AI systems’ self-reported text outputs as a means to assess their consciousness_
- **[Choice-based evaluations](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ijCseQ79SlLOi_7i38WbyzUYNfy_l-jErmRncLuKxqk/edit?tab=t.0):** _evaluate patterns in how AI systems make choices_
- **[Sentience, emotion and wellbeing based approaches](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ijCseQ79SlLOi_7i38WbyzUYNfy_l-jErmRncLuKxqk/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.1cthioxnhkk5):** _Assess functional (i.e. achieves a function, does not necessarily imply experience) aspects of emotion, sentience and wellbeing_
- **[Tests for self-representation or identification](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ijCseQ79SlLOi_7i38WbyzUYNfy_l-jErmRncLuKxqk/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.axr4isn0dzom):** _evaluate whether AI systems represent or can identify themselves_
- **[Methods for evaluating sets of indicators](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ijCseQ79SlLOi_7i38WbyzUYNfy_l-jErmRncLuKxqk/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.ahjvb2jch987):** _ways in which different indicators may be combined into assessments of consciousness or other mental states_
- **[Other approaches to consciousness tests](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ijCseQ79SlLOi_7i38WbyzUYNfy_l-jErmRncLuKxqk/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.7shnljtuj2al):** _Schneider and Turner’s Artificial Consciousness Test and Chip test, and the Turing test and related approaches._
- **[Approaches to validating tests](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ijCseQ79SlLOi_7i38WbyzUYNfy_l-jErmRncLuKxqk/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.ydfvxkqxgrzr):** _methods to check whether tests assess consciousness, or the mental state they are supposed to evaluate._

On **Slack, let your group know which section you have chosen**.

Then, read the relevant section _(should take 5 minutes)_, and **spend up to 15 minutes** noting down your thoughts on the section you read. 

1. What are the **core parts** of the approaches or methods you read about?
2. What are their **advantages** and **disadvantages** in determining consciousness or other mental states in AI systems?
3. How might these approaches **shift the probability you place** on artificial moral patienthood, if at all? Is a system more likely to pass these tests 1) if it is a moral patient or 2) if it is not a moral patient? 

If it serves your learning better, feel free to focus on **one** specific approach within the section. 

You will talk this through in a small group in the discussion - no need to reach expert level!
