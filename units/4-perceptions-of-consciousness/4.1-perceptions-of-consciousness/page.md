## Page text

**Welcome to Unit 4!**

This unit will introduce questions like:
- How and why do humans perceive AI systems to be conscious, and how does this affect humans?
- What might be the societal and ethical implications of perceptions of consciousness?

Learning outcomes:
- Explain the importance of public perceptions of digital consciousness
- Discuss potential risks from under or over attributing consciousness or sentience to AI systems
- Describe how attributing consciousness or moral patienthood to AI systems may affect the users of these systems
- Identify factors that underlie perceptions of consciousness or moral patienthood
- Recognise how widespread perceptions of consciousness or moral patienthood in AI systems are
- Describe the risk of societal division
