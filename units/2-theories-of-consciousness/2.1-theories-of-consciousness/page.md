## Page text

**Welcome to Unit 2!**

This unit will introduce questions like:
- What is consciousness and what is the state of our current understanding of it? 
- What is computational functionalism, and what are the arguments for and against it? 
- What are the main problems in studying consciousness, particularly in AI systems?

By the end of this unit, you should be able to:
- Describe the major philosophical problems related to studying subjective experience (e.g. the hard problem of consciousness, the problem of other minds, the gaming problem)
- Appreciate the current state of our understanding of consciousness
- Discuss leading theories of consciousness and how they might apply to digital minds
- Explain computational functionalism and its potential application to digital minds
- Discuss arguments for and against computational functionalism

**We hope you enjoy this Unit!** Please add any feedback inline, and reach out to online-course@digitalminds.cam with any questions.

## Exercises

### Exercise 1

*Free text · Core*

Choose one leading theory of consciousness - or condition  related to it. Spend 20 minutes learning about it and noting down the below: 
- what the theory or condition claims
- any evidence for or against it
- how it may apply to digital minds

We will discuss this in the session - you don't need to become an expert, just be ready to note the basics about your chosen theory or condition.

In your group's Slack channel, please note down which theory or idea you are looking at so we get a wide range of theories. 

Some suggestions for which to choose and where to look:
1. Global Workspace Theory: Butlin et al., '[Consciousness in Artificial Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.08708)' p.22, or Larissa Schiavo's [blogpost](https://larissaschiavo.substack.com/p/consciousness-in-ai-explained-global)
2. Biological Naturalism: Section 4 in Anil Seth’s [Conscious Artificial Intelligence and Biological Naturalism'](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/C9912A5BE9D806012E3C8B3AF612E39A/S0140525X25000032a.pdf/conscious-artificial-intelligence-and-biological-naturalism.pdf)
3. Recurrent Processing: Butlin et al., '[Consciousness in Artificial Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.08708)', p.19
4. Computational Higher Order Theories: Butlin et al., '[Consciousness in Artificial Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.08708)', p.29
5. Attention Schema Theory: Butlin et al., '[Consciousness in Artificial Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.08708)', p.33
6. Agency and Embodiment: Butlin et al., '[Consciousness in Artificial Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.08708)', p.37
