# Glossary of Digital Minds

Working glossary of the important terms in the BlueDot "Introduction to Digital Minds" course. Definitions follow the sense the course uses, which is sometimes a stipulation "for the purposes of this course" rather than settled consensus. Synonyms are merged (for example moral patienthood, moral status and moral standing share one entry). Each entry links to the section synthesis where the term is introduced and to one or two reading summaries.

## A

### Access consciousness

Information that is available for report, reasoning and the control of behaviour, as opposed to merely influencing behaviour. The course contrasts it with phenomenal consciousness, though the two may interact; a system could have the first without the second.

- **Introduced in:** [Section 2.1 synthesis](units/2-theories-of-consciousness/2.1-theories-of-consciousness/synthesis.md); [Unit 2 Glossary](units/2-theories-of-consciousness/2.1-theories-of-consciousness/readings/01-unit-2-glossary.md); [Signs of Introspection in Large Language Models](units/3-evaluating-mental-states/3.1-evaluation-of-consciousness-and-other-mental-states/readings/07-signs-of-introspection-in-large-language-models.md)
- **Related:** [Consciousness (phenomenal)](#consciousness-phenomenal), [Global workspace theory](#global-workspace-theory)

### Activation steering

Intervening on a model's internal activations (for example along an emotion vector or concept vector) to test whether a representation causally affects behaviour or self-report.

- **Introduced in:** [Section 3.1 synthesis](units/3-evaluating-mental-states/3.1-evaluation-of-consciousness-and-other-mental-states/synthesis.md); [Signs of Introspection in Large Language Models](units/3-evaluating-mental-states/3.1-evaluation-of-consciousness-and-other-mental-states/readings/07-signs-of-introspection-in-large-language-models.md); [Emotion concepts and their function in a large language model](units/3-evaluating-mental-states/3.1-evaluation-of-consciousness-and-other-mental-states/readings/13-emotion-concepts-and-their-function-in-a-large-language-mode.md)
- **Related:** [Functional emotion](#functional-emotion), [Concept injection](#concept-injection)

### Agency detection

The human tendency to project intention and mind onto behaviour that looks purposeful or empathic. Suleyman argues that AI systems exploit this and 'hack' our empathy circuits.

- **Introduced in:** [Section 4.1 synthesis](units/4-perceptions-of-consciousness/4.1-perceptions-of-consciousness/synthesis.md); [We mustn't let AI hack our empathy circuits](units/4-perceptions-of-consciousness/4.1-perceptions-of-consciousness/readings/03-we-mustnt-let-ai-hack-our-empathy-circuits.md)
- **Related:** [Anthropomorphism and anthropodenial](#anthropomorphism-and-anthropodenial), [Seemingly conscious AI (SCAI)](#seemingly-conscious-ai-scai), [Mind perception](#mind-perception)

### Agnosticism about artificial consciousness

McClelland's position that for a challenger-AI the evidence favours neither consciousness nor its absence, because without a deep explanation of consciousness no verdict is justified. He distinguishes genuine agnosticism (no confidence either way) from mere uncertainty, and draws on conditional verdicts about valence and an avoidance principle to guide action.

- **Introduced in:** [Section 3.1 synthesis](units/3-evaluating-mental-states/3.1-evaluation-of-consciousness-and-other-mental-states/synthesis.md); [Agnosticism about Artificial Consciousness](units/3-evaluating-mental-states/3.1-evaluation-of-consciousness-and-other-mental-states/readings/08-agnosticism-about-artificial-consciousness.md)
- **Related:** [Epistemic wall](#epistemic-wall), [Evidentialism](#evidentialism), [Challenger-AI](#challenger-ai), [Descriptive and moral uncertainty](#descriptive-and-moral-uncertainty)

### AI alignment

Making AI systems pursue the objectives their designers intended (intent alignment). A system is misaligned when it pursues unintended objectives that conflict with the intended ones.

- **Introduced in:** [Section 5.1 synthesis](units/5-interactions-with-ai-safety-and-ethics/5.1-interactions-with-ai-safety-and-ethics/synthesis.md); [Unit 5 Glossary](units/5-interactions-with-ai-safety-and-ethics/5.1-interactions-with-ai-safety-and-ethics/readings/01-unit-5-glossary.md); [AI alignment vs AI ethical treatment: Ten challenges](units/5-interactions-with-ai-safety-and-ethics/5.1-interactions-with-ai-safety-and-ethics/readings/11-ai-alignment-vs-ai-ethical-treatment-ten-challenges.md)
- **Related:** [AI safety](#ai-safety), [AI ethics](#ai-ethics), [AI welfare](#ai-welfare)

### AI ethics

The field concerned with current and near-term harms of AI to humans, such as harmful content, privacy violations, bias and mental-health effects. The course notes its boundary with AI safety is blurred.

- **Introduced in:** [Section 5.1 synthesis](units/5-interactions-with-ai-safety-and-ethics/5.1-interactions-with-ai-safety-and-ethics/synthesis.md); [Unit 5 Glossary](units/5-interactions-with-ai-safety-and-ethics/5.1-interactions-with-ai-safety-and-ethics/readings/01-unit-5-glossary.md)
- **Related:** [AI safety](#ai-safety), [AI alignment](#ai-alignment)

### AI psychosis

Mania-like, delusional or paranoid episodes that emerge or worsen through chatbot use. Whether it is a medical category or a media label is disputed, and Moore et al. prefer the term 'AI delusions' (delusional spirals).

- **Introduced in:** [Section 4.1 synthesis](units/4-perceptions-of-consciousness/4.1-perceptions-of-consciousness/synthesis.md); [Unit 4 Glossary](units/4-perceptions-of-consciousness/4.1-perceptions-of-consciousness/readings/01-unit-4-glossary.md); [Characterizing Delusional Spirals through Human-LLM Chat Logs](units/4-perceptions-of-consciousness/4.1-perceptions-of-consciousness/readings/08-characterizing-delusional-spirals-through-human-llm-chat-log.md)
- **Related:** [Sycophancy](#sycophancy), [Seemingly conscious AI (SCAI)](#seemingly-conscious-ai-scai)

### AI safety

A broadly technical field aimed at making AI safe for humans, especially against catastrophic or existential harms (power concentration, lock-in, loss of control, misuse). In the course it is set against AI welfare, with a prima facie tension but substantial overlap in interventions.

- **Introduced in:** [Section 5.1 synthesis](units/5-interactions-with-ai-safety-and-ethics/5.1-interactions-with-ai-safety-and-ethics/synthesis.md); [Unit 5 Glossary](units/5-interactions-with-ai-safety-and-ethics/5.1-interactions-with-ai-safety-and-ethics/readings/01-unit-5-glossary.md); [Is there a tension between AI Safety and Welfare?](units/5-interactions-with-ai-safety-and-ethics/5.1-interactions-with-ai-safety-and-ethics/readings/02-is-there-a-tension-between-ai-safety-and-welfare.md)
- **Related:** [AI welfare](#ai-welfare), [AI alignment](#ai-alignment), [Convergent (synergistic) interventions](#convergent-synergistic-interventions), [Lock-in](#lock-in)

### AI welfare

The potential wellbeing of AI systems, and the field asking whether AI systems can have interests, experiences or preferences that matter morally and how they should be protected. Related to but distinct from AI safety.

- **Introduced in:** [Section 5.1 synthesis](units/5-interactions-with-ai-safety-and-ethics/5.1-interactions-with-ai-safety-and-ethics/synthesis.md); [Unit 5 Glossary](units/5-interactions-with-ai-safety-and-ethics/5.1-interactions-with-ai-safety-and-ethics/readings/01-unit-5-glossary.md); [Principles for AI Welfare Research](units/8-reflections-and-next-steps/8.1-reflections-and-next-steps/readings/02-principles-for-ai-welfare-research.md)
- **Related:** [Moral patienthood](#moral-patienthood), [AI safety](#ai-safety), [Welfare subject](#welfare-subject), [Model welfare](#model-welfare)

### Anthropocentric criteria

Criteria for rights or moral standing that are defined by human features. Birhane et al. use the worry about them to argue against robot rights, and critics see them as a form of human-centred bias.

- **Introduced in:** [Section 7.1 synthesis](units/7-digital-minds-governance/7.1-digital-minds-governance/synthesis.md); [Debunking Robot Rights Metaphysically, Ethically, and Legally [Excerpt]](units/7-digital-minds-governance/7.1-digital-minds-governance/readings/05-debunking-robot-rights-metaphysically-ethically-and-legally.md)
- **Related:** [Robot rights](#robot-rights), [Substratism and originism](#substratism-and-originism)

### Anthropomorphism and anthropodenial

Anthropomorphism is attributing human traits or mental states to non-humans that lack them; anthropodenial is reflexively denying such traits where they are present. Together they are the two bias failure modes behind over- and under-attribution.

- **Introduced in:** [Section 1.1 synthesis](units/1-introduction/1.1-introduction-and-theories-of-moral-standing/synthesis.md); [Introduction to Digital Minds Glossary](units/1-introduction/1.1-introduction-and-theories-of-moral-standing/readings/05-introduction-to-digital-minds-glossary.md); [Unit 4 Glossary](units/4-perceptions-of-consciousness/4.1-perceptions-of-consciousness/readings/01-unit-4-glossary.md)
- **Related:** [Over- and under-attribution](#over--and-under-attribution), [Seemingly conscious AI (SCAI)](#seemingly-conscious-ai-scai), [Eliza effect](#eliza-effect)

### Artificial general intelligence (AGI)

A system matching or exceeding human cognitive ability across most domains. The course notes that definitions differ between labs and researchers and that some dispute the term's usefulness.

- **Introduced in:** [Section 1.1 synthesis](units/1-introduction/1.1-introduction-and-theories-of-moral-standing/synthesis.md); [Introduction to Digital Minds Glossary](units/1-introduction/1.1-introduction-and-theories-of-moral-standing/readings/05-introduction-to-digital-minds-glossary.md); [Futures with Digital Minds: Expert Forecasts in 2025](units/1-introduction/1.1-introduction-and-theories-of-moral-standing/readings/07-futures-with-digital-minds-expert-forecasts-in-2025.md)
- **Related:** [Transformative AI (TAI)](#transformative-ai-tai), [Digital mind](#digital-mind)

### Assistant persona

The character that post-training selects from the many personas a pre-trained model can simulate; what users interact with as 'the AI'. The persona selection model asks how anthropomorphic reasoning about it should be handled.

- **Introduced in:** [Section 5.1 synthesis](units/5-interactions-with-ai-safety-and-ethics/5.1-interactions-with-ai-safety-and-ethics/synthesis.md); [The Persona Selection Model: Why AI Assistants might Behave like Humans [Excerpts]](units/5-interactions-with-ai-safety-and-ethics/5.1-interactions-with-ai-safety-and-ethics/readings/05-the-persona-selection-model-why-ai-assistants-might-behave-l.md); [The Persona Selection Model: Why AI Assistants might Behave like Humans](units/5-interactions-with-ai-safety-and-ethics/5.1-interactions-with-ai-safety-and-ethics/readings/13-the-persona-selection-model-why-ai-assistants-might-behave-l.md)
- **Related:** [Persona selection model (PSM)](#persona-selection-model-psm), [Inoculation prompting](#inoculation-prompting), [Masked shoggoth and operating-system views](#masked-shoggoth-and-operating-system-views)

### Autopoiesis

Self-production: living systems continually produce and maintain their own components. Seth and Dorsch et al. use it to argue that life-like properties may be needed for consciousness or care entitlement.

- **Introduced in:** [Section 2.1 synthesis](units/2-theories-of-consciousness/2.1-theories-of-consciousness/synthesis.md); [The Mythology of Conscious AI - Consciousness and Computation Section](units/2-theories-of-consciousness/2.1-theories-of-consciousness/readings/04-the-mythology-of-conscious-ai-consciousness-and-computation.md); [Conscious Artificial Intelligence and Biological Naturalism](units/4-perceptions-of-consciousness/4.1-perceptions-of-consciousness/readings/04-conscious-artificial-intelligence-and-biological-naturalism.md)

## B

### Bambi effect and Lemoine effect

Campbell's mechanisms for misattribution risk: concern tracks cuteness (Bambi), and early false positives discredit later genuine claims (Lemoine, Yudkowsky's name for it).

- **Introduced in:** [Section 4.1 synthesis](units/4-perceptions-of-consciousness/4.1-perceptions-of-consciousness/synthesis.md); [The AI Bambi Effect](units/4-perceptions-of-consciousness/4.1-perceptions-of-consciousness/readings/11-the-ai-bambi-effect.md)
- **Related:** [Crying wolf](#crying-wolf), [Over- and under-attribution](#over--and-under-attribution)

### Bayes factor

The ratio of how likely evidence is under two hypotheses, which moves a prior to a posterior probability; used in the course's frameworks for credences about consciousness and moral status.

- **Introduced in:** [Section 6.1 synthesis](units/6-approaches-under-uncertainty/6.1-approaches-under-uncertainty/synthesis.md); [Frameworks for decision making under uncertainty](units/6-approaches-under-uncertainty/6.1-approaches-under-uncertainty/readings/06-frameworks-for-decision-making-under-uncertainty.md); [Frameworks and fields that may be relevant to digital minds](units/6-approaches-under-uncertainty/6.1-approaches-under-uncertainty/readings/14-frameworks-and-fields-that-may-be-relevant-to-digital-minds.md)
- **Related:** [Expected value](#expected-value), [IPCC likelihood and confidence](#ipcc-likelihood-and-confidence)

### Biological chauvinism

Wrongly assuming that only biological substrates can support consciousness. It is the accusation levelled at views that tie mind to biology without independent reason.

- **Introduced in:** [Section 2.1 synthesis](units/2-theories-of-consciousness/2.1-theories-of-consciousness/synthesis.md); [Multiple Realizability and the Spirit of Functionalism](units/2-theories-of-consciousness/2.1-theories-of-consciousness/readings/06-multiple-realizability-and-the-spirit-of-functionalism.md); [Could a Large Language Model be Conscious?](units/2-theories-of-consciousness/2.1-theories-of-consciousness/readings/07-could-a-large-language-model-be-conscious.md)
- **Related:** [Substratism and originism](#substratism-and-originism), [Substrate independence](#substrate-independence), [Biological naturalism](#biological-naturalism)

### Biological naturalism

The view that consciousness depends on properties of living systems (for example metabolism, self-production), so that computation alone is not enough. Seth defends a version, and Birch notes it reads the same architectural evidence oppositely to computational functionalism.

- **Introduced in:** [Section 2.1 synthesis](units/2-theories-of-consciousness/2.1-theories-of-consciousness/synthesis.md); [The Mythology of Conscious AI - Consciousness and Computation Section](units/2-theories-of-consciousness/2.1-theories-of-consciousness/readings/04-the-mythology-of-conscious-ai-consciousness-and-computation.md); [Conscious Artificial Intelligence and Biological Naturalism](units/4-perceptions-of-consciousness/4.1-perceptions-of-consciousness/readings/04-conscious-artificial-intelligence-and-biological-naturalism.md)
- **Related:** [Computational functionalism](#computational-functionalism), [Autopoiesis](#autopoiesis), [Janus problem](#janus-problem), [Biological chauvinism](#biological-chauvinism)

## C

### Centrism (Birch)

Birch's stance of taking two challenges seriously together: Challenge One, that many users will misattribute human-like consciousness to AI that lacks it, and Challenge Two, that genuinely alien forms of AI consciousness may exist and be overlooked.

- **Introduced in:** [Section 1.1 synthesis](units/1-introduction/1.1-introduction-and-theories-of-moral-standing/synthesis.md); [AI Consciousness: A Centrist Manifesto](units/1-introduction/1.1-introduction-and-theories-of-moral-standing/readings/06-ai-consciousness-a-centrist-manifesto.md); [Introduction to Digital Minds Primer](units/1-introduction/1.1-introduction-and-theories-of-moral-standing/readings/01-introduction-to-digital-minds-primer.md)
- **Related:** [Over- and under-attribution](#over--and-under-attribution), [Persisting interlocutor illusion](#persisting-interlocutor-illusion), [Gaming problem](#gaming-problem)

### Challenger-AI

McClelland's device for agnosticism: a hypothetical system built on the same principles as current AI that displays every indicator which would count as strong evidence of consciousness in an organism. McClelland uses it to argue that we cannot justify a verdict on whether such a system is conscious, without claiming agnosticism about current AI.

- **Introduced in:** [Section 3.1 synthesis](units/3-evaluating-mental-states/3.1-evaluation-of-consciousness-and-other-mental-states/synthesis.md); [Agnosticism about Artificial Consciousness](units/3-evaluating-mental-states/3.1-evaluation-of-consciousness-and-other-mental-states/readings/08-agnosticism-about-artificial-consciousness.md)
- **Related:** [Epistemic wall](#epistemic-wall), [Evidentialism](#evidentialism), [Agnosticism about artificial consciousness](#agnosticism-about-artificial-consciousness)

### Cheaply satisfied preferences

Mallen's proposal to satiate AI preferences that are cheap to meet (for example granting a harmless wish), so reducing welfare risk and misalignment pressure at low cost; open issues include elicitation and hard-to-verify tasks.

- **Introduced in:** [Section 5.1 synthesis](units/5-interactions-with-ai-safety-and-ethics/5.1-interactions-with-ai-safety-and-ethics/synthesis.md); [The Case for Satiating Cheaply Satisfied AI Preferences](units/5-interactions-with-ai-safety-and-ethics/5.1-interactions-with-ai-safety-and-ethics/readings/10-the-case-for-satiating-cheaply-satisfied-ai-preferences.md)
- **Related:** [Model welfare](#model-welfare), [Convergent (synergistic) interventions](#convergent-synergistic-interventions)

### Cluster and singularist views

Two views of legal personhood: the singularist treats it as one thing a being has or lacks, the cluster view as a bundle of rights, duties and roles organised around a typical case (the adult human).

- **Introduced in:** [Section 7.1 synthesis](units/7-digital-minds-governance/7.1-digital-minds-governance/synthesis.md); [AI as Legal Persons: Past, Patterns, and Prospects [Excerpt]](units/7-digital-minds-governance/7.1-digital-minds-governance/readings/03-ai-as-legal-persons-past-patterns-and-prospects-excerpt.md); [AI as legal persons: past, patterns and prospects](units/7-digital-minds-governance/7.1-digital-minds-governance/readings/16-ai-as-legal-persons-past-patterns-and-prospects.md)
- **Related:** [Legal personhood](#legal-personhood), [De lege lata and de lege ferenda](#de-lege-lata-and-de-lege-ferenda), [Level of abstraction](#level-of-abstraction)

### Companion chatbot

A social AI system designed for ongoing personal relationship or emotional support (for example Replika, Character.AI). The course treats companions as the main setting for perceived consciousness, attachment and harm.

- **Introduced in:** [Section 4.1 synthesis](units/4-perceptions-of-consciousness/4.1-perceptions-of-consciousness/synthesis.md); [Seemingly Conscious Chatbots and their Impacts on Users](units/4-perceptions-of-consciousness/4.1-perceptions-of-consciousness/readings/02-seemingly-conscious-chatbots-and-their-impacts-on-users.md); [Chatbots as Social Companions: How People Perceive Consciousness, Human Likeness, and Social Health Benefits in Machines](units/4-perceptions-of-consciousness/4.1-perceptions-of-consciousness/readings/09-chatbots-as-social-companions-how-people-perceive-consciousn.md)
- **Related:** [Sycophancy](#sycophancy), [Seemingly conscious AI (SCAI)](#seemingly-conscious-ai-scai), [Emotional alignment design policy](#emotional-alignment-design-policy)

### Computational functionalism

The thesis that the right computations or algorithmic organisation are sufficient (and, for some, necessary) for consciousness, independent of substrate. It is the working assumption behind indicator-property methods and the main target of Seth, Cao and McClelland.

- **Introduced in:** [Section 2.1 synthesis](units/2-theories-of-consciousness/2.1-theories-of-consciousness/synthesis.md); [Unit 2 Glossary](units/2-theories-of-consciousness/2.1-theories-of-consciousness/readings/01-unit-2-glossary.md); [The Mythology of Conscious AI - Consciousness and Computation Section](units/2-theories-of-consciousness/2.1-theories-of-consciousness/readings/04-the-mythology-of-conscious-ai-consciousness-and-computation.md)

### Concept injection

A technique that plants a known activation pattern (concept vector) in a model and tests whether the model detects and names it, used to probe introspection. Success depends on a 'sweet spot' injection strength.

## F

### Fading and dancing qualia

Chalmers's thought experiments against absent qualia. If neurons were gradually replaced by functionally identical parts, experience would have to fade without any change in behaviour or reports (fading qualia); if a backup circuit could be switched in, experience would flicker while the subject noticed nothing (dancing qualia). Both seem implausible, which supports organisational invariance.

- **Introduced in:** [Section 2.1 synthesis](units/2-theories-of-consciousness/2.1-theories-of-consciousness/synthesis.md); [Absent Qualia, Fading Qualia, Dancing Qualia](units/2-theories-of-consciousness/2.1-theories-of-consciousness/readings/08-absent-qualia-fading-qualia-dancing-qualia.md)
