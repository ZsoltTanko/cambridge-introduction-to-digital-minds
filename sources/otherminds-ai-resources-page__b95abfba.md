# otherminds.ai | AI Welfare, Sentience and Cognition Research

## Resources

Tabs: Organizations (10), Fellowships (5), Grants (3), Courses (1), Events (6), Newsletters (1).

### Organizations

- **Eleos AI Research** (https://eleosai.org/): Nonprofit dedicated to understanding wellbeing and moral patienthood of AI systems. Tags: AI welfare, Moral status, AI cognition.
- **NYU Center for Mind Ethics and Policy (CMEP)** (https://nonhumanminds.org/): Academic center researching the moral status of nonhuman minds including AI and invertebrates led by Jeff Sebo. Tags: Moral status, Consciousness, Philosophy.
- **PRISM (Partnership for Research Into Sentient Machines)** (https://www.prism-global.com/): UK nonprofit investigating sentient machines. Tags: Consciousness, AI welfare.
- **California Institute for Machine Consciousness (CIMC)** (https://cimc.ai/): New 2025 institute researching machine consciousness including work by Joscha Bach on consciousness hypotheses. Tags: Consciousness, Philosophy, AI cognition.
- **Conscium** (https://conscium.com/): Research org promoting responsible approaches to conscious AI. Tags: Consciousness, AI welfare, AI rights.
- **Sentient Futures**: Field building organization that runs courses, project incubators, residencies, and conferences. Focused on building better futures for all sentient beings, including artificial minds and animals, in a time of rapid technological development. Tags: AI welfare, Moral status.
- **Rethink Priorities**: Research nonprofit with a dedicated worldview investigations team refining models of digital consciousness and moral weight. Tags: Consciousness, Moral status, AI welfare.
- **Anthropic Model Welfare Program**: Internal research team led by Kyle Fish evaluating welfare and moral status of Claude models. Tags: AI welfare, AI emotions, Moral status.
- **Leverhulme Centre for the Future of Intelligence (LCFI)**: Cambridge center housing the Consciousness and Intelligence program investigating consciousness emergence in AI systems. Tags: Consciousness, AI cognition, Philosophy.
- **Center for the Future of AI Mind & Society (FAU)**: Organizes MindFest focused on sentience autonomy and human-AI interaction. Tags: Consciousness, AI cognition.

### Fellowships

- **Future Impact Group (FIG)**: Runs research fellowships including projects on AI sentience, welfare and digital minds. Tags: AI welfare, AI cognition.
- **Anthropic Fellows Program**: 4-month paid fellowship including a dedicated Model Welfare workstream. Tags: AI welfare, Alignment, Interpretability.
- **Pivotal Research Fellowship**: 9-week London-based fellowship covering AI safety including AI welfare topics. Tags: AI welfare, Safety, Alignment.
- **ERA Fellowship (Cambridge)**: Summer fellowship for technical and governance AI safety research includes options for digital minds projects. Tags: Safety, AI welfare, AI cognition.
- **MATS (ML Alignment & Theory Scholars)**: Research training program; some scholars work on welfare-adjacent topics like interpretability and model cognition. Tags: Alignment, Interpretability, AI cognition.

### Grants

- **Longview Philanthropy: Digital Sentience Fund**: Funds research and public education on consciousness sentience and moral status of AI. Tags: AI welfare, Consciousness, Moral status.
- **Coefficient Giving**: Supports digital minds work as a focus area expanding grantmaking in coming years. Tags: AI welfare, Moral status.
- **Survival and Flourishing Fund (SFF)**: Support to multiple AI welfare and digital minds organizations. Tags: AI welfare, Safety.

### Courses

- **Digital Minds Course**: An 8-week online course exploring the perceptions and plausibility of digital minds - and what this may mean for ethics and society. Tags: Consciousness, Safety, Moral status.

### Events

- **NYU Mind Ethics and Policy Summit**: Summit on consciousness sentience moral and legal status of nonhumans including AI and invertebrates. Tags: Consciousness, Moral status, AI rights.
- **Sentient Futures Summit**: Conferences in San Francisco and London bringing together digital minds, animal welfare, and AI safety communities to create a better future for all sentient beings. Tags: AI welfare, Moral status.
- **Conference on AI Consciousness and Welfare**: Organized by Eleos AI Research gathering researchers on AI sentience evaluation. Tags: Consciousness, AI welfare.
- **MindFest (FAU)**: Annual event on machine consciousness sentience and the future of human-AI interaction. Tags: Consciousness, AI cognition.
- **AISB Convention (University of Sussex)**: Society for AI and Simulation of Behaviour convention with Anil Seth as keynote; digital minds topics welcomed. Tags: AI cognition, Consciousness, Philosophy.
- **ICCS AI and Sentience Conference**: International Center for Consciousness Studies event featuring talks from Chalmers, Blackmore and Hulme on AI consciousness. Tags: Consciousness, Philosophy.
