# Digital Minds Governance Table Editor

Near-term profiles of different kinds of digital minds governance

Instruction shown on the page: "Click any cell to edit"

## Columns

1. Kind of governance
2. Design difficulty
3. Implementation difficulty
4. Political prospects
5. Competent attempts' expected impact on digital minds
6. Relation to safety

## Rows

### Preventive
Prevent the creation of digital minds.

### Protective
Protect the basic interests of digital minds.

### Integrative
Integrate digital minds into society.

## Drop-down options

Design difficulty and Implementation difficulty:
- Easy
- Moderate
- Hard
- Very Hard
- Likely infeasible

Political prospects:
- Excellent
- Good
- Dicey
- Poor
- Bleak

Competent attempts' expected impact on digital minds, and Relation to safety:
- Strongly net positive
- Mildly net positive
- Neutral/ambiguous
- Mildly net negative
- Strongly net negative

Each drop-down begins with an empty "Select..." option, and cells show "Click to select" until a value is chosen.

## Notes

- Cell colours are keyed to the option chosen (for example Easy is light green, Likely infeasible is red, Excellent is dark green, Bleak is red).
- In the Integrative row, the Implementation difficulty cell is merged into the Design difficulty cell and cannot be edited.
- The page has an "Export as Image" button that renders the table to a PNG.
- The table title links to: https://meditationsondigitalminds.substack.com/p/three-kinds-of-digital-minds-governance
