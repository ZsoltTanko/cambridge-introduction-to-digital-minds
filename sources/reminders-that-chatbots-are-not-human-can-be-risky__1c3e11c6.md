Reminders that chatbots are not human can be risky.

Laestadius LI(1), Campos-Castillo C(2).

Author information:
(1)Zilber College of Public Health, University of Wisconsin-Milwaukee, 
Milwaukee, WI, USA. Electronic address: LLaestad@uwm.edu.
(2)Department of Media and Information, Michigan State University, East Lansing, 
MI, USA.

Concerns about mental and physical health harms from chatbots are prompting 
policies mandating ongoing reminders that chatbots are not human. While 
well-intended, evidence suggests that reminders may be either ineffective or 
harmful to users. Discovering how to best remind people that chatbots are not 
human is a critical research priority.
